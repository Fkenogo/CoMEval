## Earlier product framing

CoMEval was proposed as a shared system for project-configured plans, activity and measurement capture, evidence review, reporting and learning. The prototype organized that experience as Plan → Do / Measure → Prove → Report → Learn.

These are useful experience-reference areas. They do not yet establish the final product workflow or where the primary user problem lies.

## Candidate principles to revisit

- Keep project ownership and access scoped to each project.
- Connect reported results to the source records and evidence that support them.
- Preserve issued reports when later approved corrections change live reporting.
- Clearly distinguish a measure from its target and preserve the selected period’s context.
- Reuse or compare data across related projects only when definitions, populations, methods and contexts are sufficiently comparable.
- Establish what review and approval mean for each source type and what the system can verify.

These points are candidates for the CoMEval Constitution and product foundation. They remain open for discussion and refinement.

## Prototype-derived reporting requirements

The prototype walkthrough established useful behaviours to retain for review:

- A pending correction leaves the live result unchanged. An approved correction updates live reporting.
- An issued report keeps its original figures and evidence references after a correction.
- The tested experience displayed selected-period attainment and the final programme target separately.
- A report should provide access to the exact evidence version it cites for an authorized viewer.

Review these behaviours against the problem definition before adopting them as product requirements. See the Master Programme for the current prototype verification status and known gaps.
