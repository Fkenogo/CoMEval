# CME-001 — Product Foundation v0.2

CoMEval serves funded programmes that collect field evidence and report results against agreed indicators. Miledge Ventures maintains one shared product. A project configures its framework, sites, activities, indicators, instrument versions, baselines, period and final targets, and evidence requirements. Onboard through guided setup. The two initial use cases both inform the product from the start. Their rollout may be staggered according to readiness.

## Workflow

Plan → Do / Measure → Prove → Report → Learn. A programme lead approves a versioned plan. Field staff capture approved instruments and evidence. Reviewers verify or return records with reasons. Authorised approvers accept records, and authorised staff issue reports. Track follow-up findings with an owner and due date. Keep the Guide and add short instructions during capture, evidence upload, correction and reporting. AI navigation and Q&A are later enhancements.

## Project owner access

Membership is explicit, scoped to a project and denied by default. A project owner can access their project according to agreed permissions. Access to report figures, underlying records, evidence and export may differ by role. Enforce the boundary in server-side reads, writes, search, reports, file access and exports. Staff who legitimately work across projects receive separate assignments and operate within one selected project at a time. Set access revocation and any support access with each owner.

## Reporting contract

- Issuing freezes the reporting period, plan version, approved record revisions, calculation inputs and figures, and evidence version IDs. Authorised viewers can open the exact cited file version. Store enough citation metadata to explain unavailable evidence according to the agreed retention policy.
- Pending corrections leave live figures unchanged. Approval updates live figures while the original issued report remains reproducible. A corrected issue has a new identity, date and reason, and links to its original.
- Calculate attainment using the selected period's approved target where applicable. Label the final programme target separately. Show missing, zero or otherwise non-comparable targets explicitly according to approved indicator rules.

## Before partner onboarding

Agree each project's offline capture needs, permitted devices and files, evidence access, retention and withdrawal, operational sponsor, and intended report. Start verification with fictional fixtures. Use project materials only within authorised project access and approved handling.
