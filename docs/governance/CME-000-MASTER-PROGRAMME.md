## Working method

1. Work one problem or decision at a time in conversation with the Founder.
2. Record lived experience, source evidence, assumptions, options, and open questions distinctly.
3. Use ChildFund and Omugo as separate design-partner contexts. Test shared needs and differences rather than assume a single workflow.
4. Maintain a decision log as decisions are made. Do not turn a discussion proposal into product policy without Founder confirmation.
5. Use the prototype to inspect and refine experience. Its current screens and rules do not dictate the final product.
6. Bring in coding agents only for specifically authorized prototype reconciliation or engineering work. Product exploration and document drafting continue directly with the Founder.
7. Before any coding assignment, follow AGENTS.md and complete START-01.

## Founder-described opportunity

Teams often collect implementation reports, activity records, feedback, survey results and evaluation material at different times and in different places. When evaluation happens later, teams may have difficulty reconstructing what happened, who and where activities reached, and which source supports a reported result. Related projects may repeat measures but manage baselines, tools, reports and trends separately.

The opportunity under exploration is a digital M&E platform that builds a traceable picture during implementation, connects data and reports to their sources, supports review and approval, and helps successive projects learn from comparable prior measures. See Problem Exploration. This is an opportunity statement, not a settled scope.
